MyPeople Features

Book a Call

Site Designer

From the comfort of a browser, create the community site of your dreams, with complete control over branding, layout, CSS and design to suit your specific objectives.

Example Use Case: Create a full website that aligns with your existing brand and build a community that your audience will recognize and trust.

Content Management System

Effortlessly create, manage, and share rich, SEO-optimized content effortlessly with our AI-enhanced WYSIWYG editor. Intuitive navigation and menus help curate your site.

Example Use Case: Share company updates, articles, and member-generated content to keep your community engaged and informed.

CRM

Complete system to track and score member interactions. It's an essential tool to enable your sales team and product managers to gain insight into member behavior, identify leads and close sales.

Example Use Case: Tailor communication and outreach based on member activity and interests.

Email Campaign Management

Makes it easy to reach 100% of your community with campaigns. Target your sends by segmenting your members with demographic-specific queries.

Example Use Case: Send newsletters, event invitations, and updates direct to member's inboxes.and trust.

Drive and File Manager

Organize images, documents and resources for your community site in one easy-to-navigate area.

Example Use Case: Share important documents and manage community assets.

Virtual Events & Webinars

Not only is it easy to host live events to engage and educate members, but MyPeople also offers virtual trade show floors, booths, auditoriums, video chats and one-on-one meetings.

Example Use Case: Create a series of webinars that cater to your member's interests. and trust.

Metrics & Analytics Dashboard

Gain insights into community engagement and growth through customizable dashboards, graphics and reports.

Example Use Case: Analyze member activity to identify trends and optimize content strategies.

Customizable User Profiles

Allow members to showcase their skills, interests and expertise. Blockchain technology protects and validates ownership of profiles.

Example Use Case: Foster deeper and more frequent member interactions and connections.

Blogs & Discussion Forums

Encourage knowledge sharing and conversations among members through content and threaded discussions. Built-in AI facilitates answers and engagement.

Example Use Case: A dedicated space for Q&A, tips, and peer-to-peer support.

Learning Management System

Organize educational content and courses with a library of SCORM-compliant resources, including internal or third-party certification.

Example Use Case: Build paid or free courses, such as ones that provide specific certifications or credits to members.

Directories

You can create directories of companies, products, services, associations and more on MyPeople, complete with reviews, ratings and customer feedback features.

Example Use Case: Present users with ranked products relating to your site's primary interest.

Online Forms & Polls

Gather valuable feedback directly from members and generate leads through customizable forms, polls and quizzes.

Example Use Case: Conduct surveys to understand community needs and preferences, and integrate form submissions with your CRM.

Gamification

Redeemable points, badging and leaderboards enable you to drive more engagement through rewards and recognition.

Example Use Case: Reward members for contributing content, participating in events, and taking other actions in your community.

Storefront

Organize educational content and courses with a library of SCORM-compliant resources, including internal or third-party certification.

Example Use Case: Offer exclusive products or services directly to your members.

Affiliate Program

Drive additional revenue and member engagement by enabling your members to refer your products to their networks, and benefit via earning a percentage of any sales or points.

Example Use Case: Implement affiliate links for members to share products they love.

Online Advertising

Popular community sites can be extremely attractive to advertisers. MyPeople has a built-in system to manage ad placement and scheduling on your site.

Example Use Case: Offer advertising options to companies seeking to reach your members.

Moderation Tools & User Roles

Moderation system helps you ensure that your community site consistently provides a safe, positive and welcoming environment for your members.

Example Use Case: Empower trusted members to help moderate discussions.
