Do Not Sell My Data

MyPeople’s BHAG (Big Hairy Audacious Goal) is:

By 2040, we will impact 50% of the global workforce by empowering HR professionals in their careers; maximizing human potential.

We believe that in order for HR Professionals to have a maximum impact on human potential, they need to have the proper tools and solutions to get the job done. As such, one of mypeople.ai’s company initiatives is to connect HR professionals with the latest and greatest solutions from HR Solution Providers.In doing so, mypeople.ai may share your personal data with our valued sponsors. 

In order for mypeople.ai to no longer share your personal information with third-parties, you will no longer comply with the Terms of Agreement of our website, which will result in the deletion of your account with mypeople.ai.

We will be absolutely heartbroken if you decide to leave us - but understand your decision to do so.
